About US

Our Mission

Money shouldn’t feel confusing, intimidating, or reserved for “financial experts.” At Breathe, our mission is simple: to make financial education clear, relatable, and accessible for everyone. We believe that when people understand how money works, they gain confidence, freedom, and control over their future.

Founder

"I started Breathe because I know exactly what it feels like to step into adulthood and realize no one ever taught you how money actually works. I remember navigating credit, loans, budgeting, and major purchases with nothing but stress and scattered information, and it became clear that the problem wasn’t me, it was the way financial education is delivered. It’s often confusing, full of jargon, and disconnected from real life, even though these are the skills that shape our futures. Breathe is the resource I wish I had: a place where financial topics are broken down clearly, where you never feel judged for what you don’t know, and where learning feels empowering instead of overwhelming. My goal is to help you understand your money so you can make confident decisions, feel in control of your financial journey, and finally breathe a little easier knowing you have the knowledge you deserve." - Nayllah Buteau (Founder)

Core Values

    Clarity

    Empowerment

    Accessibility
