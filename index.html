Sooo...What is Breathe?

Glad you asked! Breathe serves as a financial academic hub for young adults approaching adulthood, or anyone looking for a place to start their financial journey. We understand that personal finance can be stressful, especially when everyone seems to have a different opinion about how money “should” work. We’re here to remind you that it doesn’t have to feel that way. Money shouldn’t be stressful. Breathe creates a calm, clear space where financial basics are explained simply, without the extra confusing jargon. At the end of the day, we want to help you build the confidence and clarity you need to make informed decisions about YOUR money.
